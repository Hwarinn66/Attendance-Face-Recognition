## Known limitations / open items

* PAD thresholds are uncalibrated defaults; evaluate on site data before production (SECURITY.md).
* Browser capture cannot prove a physical camera; digital injection bypasses passive PAD.
* Head pose from 5 landmarks is coarse; real-user false-reject rate of the challenge is not measured.
* Docker images, compose stack and CI have not been executed.
* ArcFace backend is implemented and selectable, but its weights were not downloaded or evaluated here
  (non-commercial licence).
